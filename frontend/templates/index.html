How It Works

A transparent, step-by-step process from complaint submission to resolution.

📝
Step 1
Submit

Student reports the issue with location, category, and a description.

🔍
Step 2
Review

Admin reviews the complaint, sets priority, and assigns to staff.

👷
Step 3
Work

Staff receives the task, works on it, and updates the status live.

✅
Step 4
Resolved

Student tracks progress in real-time and gives feedback on resolution.

Complaint Status Flow

Every complaint passes through clearly defined stages.

Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed
